R1 Creations

Small apps for the Rabbit R1. Do not use the R1's normal camera (double press of the side button). That camera only describes what it sees. Installs go through the creations card.

  1. On the R1, scroll to the creations card and tap it.
  2. Open the create tab and tap add via QR code.
  3. Point the R1 at the code, 6 to 10 inches from the screen, with this screen's brightness turned up. Hold still for a few seconds.
  4. Accept the install. Installed creations are at the bottom of the R1's card stack.
